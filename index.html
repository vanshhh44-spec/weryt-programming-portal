Arduino AVR

Arduino UNO / Nano

Classic UNO/Nano boards require a separate AVR-compatible uploader. They cannot be flashed by the ESP installer shown above.

We'll implement this separately after confirming exact board and bootloader variants.
Safety and compatibilityFlashing replaces the current program and may erase settings. Use the correct firmware for your exact board. Disconnect motors and external motor power while programming where practical; reconnect power only after checking wiring. Never power motors from the ESP8266's 3.3 V pin.
